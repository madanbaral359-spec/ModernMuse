What we build

From a single building to infrastructure that serves a whole community, we handle civil works from site preparation to handover.

Building construction

Residential, commercial and institutional buildings, built to approved drawings and the Nepal National Building Code.

Hydropower

Civil works for run-of-river projects: intake, headrace, settling basin, penstock supports and powerhouse.

Irrigation

Canals, headworks, lining and control structures that bring reliable water to farmland.

Road construction

Earthwork, drainage, retaining walls, culverts, bridges and surfacing for rural and urban roads.

Water supply

Intake structures, reservoir tanks, pipelines and distribution systems for drinking water.

Other civil works

Retaining and protection walls, river training, drainage and site development for public and private clients.

How we work

Every project follows the same four stages, so you know what happens and when.

Survey and plan

We study the site, drawings and quantities, then agree on scope, schedule and cost.

Build

Our engineers and site teams work to specification, with materials tested before use.

Inspect

Quality and safety checks happen at each stage, not only at the end.

Hand over

We deliver on schedule with completed records, so the structure is ready to use.
